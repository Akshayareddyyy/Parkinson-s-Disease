## 1. Non-Negotiable Fairness Mandates

To eliminate the risk of conference reviewer rejections concerning unequal comparisons, Phase 4B enforces the following strict parity constraints:

### 1.1 Identical Dataset & Cryptographic Verification
- **Dataset File:** `data/parkinsons_updrs.csv`
- **SHA-256 Checksum:** `927b23aebd30d1f11022b32c7da90a6f209122834e4c28ffe60146047663db0c`
- **Integrity Constraints:** Exactly 5,875 rows, 42 unique subjects, zero missing values, zero synthetic interpolations.

### 1.2 Exact Subject-Wise Partition Parity
- All experiments (Centralized, Feature Engineered, Federated, and Personalized) must execute on the **exact 10 audited subject-wise partitions** generated by `common.make_sets(df, seed)` for seeds $0$ through $9$:
  - **Development Cohort ($\mathcal{D}_s$):** Exactly 26 subjects ($N \ge 3,517$ records).
  - **Validation Cohort ($\mathcal{V}_s$):** Exactly 7 subjects ($N \approx 1,090$ records).
  - **Locked-Test Cohort ($\mathcal{T}_s$):** Exactly 9 subjects ($N \approx 1,268$ records).
- **Partition Disjointness Guarantee:**
  $$\mathcal{D}_s \cap \mathcal{V}_s = \emptyset, \quad \mathcal{D}_s \cap \mathcal{T}_s = \emptyset, \quad \mathcal{V}_s \cap \mathcal{T}_s = \emptyset \quad \forall s \in \{0, \dots, 9\}$$
- Under no circumstances may random recording-level splits be used for any model or experiment.

### 1.3 Full Development Data Mandate (100% Record Utilization)
- Every eligible development recording must be utilized during model training across all methodologies:
  - Centralized ML: $N_{\text{train}} = |\mathcal{D}_s| \ge 3,517$ records.
  - Federated Learning: $\sum_{k=1}^K N_{k, s} = |\mathcal{D}_s| \ge 3,517$ records.
  - Zero downsampling, zero arbitrary record truncation, zero dropped subjects.

### 1.4 Feature Space Controls
- **Permitted Predictors (19 Features):** `age`, `sex`, `test_time`, and the 16 voice dysphonia measures.
- **Strictly Excluded Columns:**
  - `subject#`: Used exclusively as grouping vector for cross-validation and client allocation; strictly excluded from feature matrix $X$.
  - `total_UPDRS`: Excluded from all models to prevent clinical target leakage.
- **Feature Engineering Parity:** Any feature engineering experiment (Exp 3) that expands or selects features must benchmark its performance against the exact 19-feature baseline on the identical subject splits.

### 1.5 Strict Separation of Prediction Targets
Models must be evaluated strictly within their designated clinical task:
- **Task A (Binary Motor Severity Classification):** Target $y = \mathbf{1}[\text{motor\_UPDRS} \ge \tau_s]$, where threshold $\tau_s = \text{median}(\text{motor\_UPDRS} \mid \mathcal{D}_s)$ is frozen on development data.
- **Task B (Continuous Motor Progression Regression):** Target continuous `motor_UPDRS` $\in [5.0377, 39.5110]$.
- **Task C (Personalized Longitudinal Delta Prediction):** Target $\Delta y_{s, t} = y_{s, t} - \bar{y}_{s, \text{base}}$ evaluated strictly for $t > k$.
- **Rule:** Never compare classification metrics against regression metrics or absolute UPDRS error against progression delta.

### 1.6 Matched Model Comparisons (Federated & Centralized)
- For every federated algorithm evaluated in Experiment 4, an exact matched centralized counterpart must be trained on the identical pooled development data.
- Distributed decision tree soft-voting must be transparently designated as **Client-Ensembled Distributed Forest (CED-Forest)** and not conflated with parameter-level FedAvg.

## 2. Evaluation Metrics Suite

All models across all experiments will be evaluated using the identical comprehensive metric suite:

### 2.1 Classification Metrics
1. Receiver Operating Characteristic Area Under Curve (ROC-AUC)
2. Precision-Recall Area Under Curve (PR-AUC)
3. Balanced Accuracy ($\frac{\text{Sensitivity} + \text{Specificity}}{2}$)
4. Sensitivity (Recall) & Specificity
5. Precision & Macro-Averaged F1-Score ($F_1$)
6. Matthews Correlation Coefficient (MCC)
7. Confusion Matrix ($\text{TN}, \text{FP}, \text{FN}, \text{TP}$)

### 2.2 Regression Metrics
1. Mean Absolute Error (MAE)
2. Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE)
3. Coefficient of Determination ($R^2$)
4. Pearson Correlation Coefficient ($r$)
5. Spearman Rank Correlation ($\rho$)
6. Subject-Macro Mean MAE & Inter-Subject Error Standard Deviation

---

## 3. Scientific Acceptance Standard

A model or pipeline will be classified as a validated improvement only if:
1. It achieves superior development/validation metrics without test leakage.
2. Its test performance improves consistently across subject partitions.
3. Its matched comparison satisfies full experimental parity.
4. Its statistical uncertainty intervals and computational costs are transparently disclosed.
